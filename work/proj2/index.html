Region One Branding & Templates

With my marketing team, we collaborated on our branding packet, making sure general staff was knowledgeable about colors, fonts, and logo usage. My key contribution was creating custom web templates for workshops and conferences.

Staff previously would spin up Google Sites without branding guidance. I devised a set of templates they could choose from depending on their event size and budget.

This led to fewer rogue Google Sites, easier processes when updating sites, and faster spin-up time.

Why we created these templates

We learned a valuable lesson early on when a department contracted its own website for conferences and services. We later asked the contractor if we could retrieve the company materials and ownership of the site.

The contractor could no longer access it after moving states and misplacing the login credentials. The department also had not paid for outright ownership, leaving us with no practical way to retrieve what we needed.
